## 是做什么的（如实复述，不评价）

把截图或视频作为"屏幕内容"，放进 3D 设备模型里（iPhone / Android / iPad），再加上场景、光照、相机、装饰 3D 物件，导出静态图或带运动的短视频。

核心动作流（官网明示）：**Build → Presets → Add Objects → Save → Export**。

最关键的产品承诺是"全部在浏览器侧完成"：你的媒体文件不离开本机，没有水印，没有账号，没有服务器上传。

## 解决什么问题（事实层面，不判断值不值得解）

- **App 营销图/视频的设备展示需求**：独立开发者做 Product Hunt / App Store 上架页时，需要把截图放进手机模型里
- **去水印/去账号门槛**：截图型工具近年的趋势是锁高级功能到付费 tier，Sliick 把"无水印+无账号+免费"作为差异化承诺（Alexandra Protsenko 在 PH 评论里专门点出"现在很少见了"）
- **绕开 After Effects / Rotato 的学习成本和渲染时间**：Maker 在回应 Curious Kitty 时明确表态，定位不是重型 3D 合成，而是"几分钟出一个 hero 图/launch post/store screenshot/short looping clip"

## 怎么做的（技术原理/机制，事实层面）

- **3D 渲染层**：Three.js + WebGL，场景在浏览器 tab 里跑（Maker 在评论里直接说明）
- **视频导出层**：MediaRecorder API 直接录制 canvas，输出 MP4（H.264，支持时）或 WebM
- **导出规格**：最长 60 秒、最多 1080p（Maker 在回复 Alexandra 时给出具体数字）
- **设备库**：iPhone / Android / iPad 三类，支持 portrait / landscape 切换
- **iPhone 配色**：Titanium / Midnight / Porcelain / Gold / Rose / Blue / Sage / Lavender / Coral 九种（基本对应 Apple iPhone 15/16 系列官方色板）
- **屏幕适配控件**：Top padding（0%）、Fit below front facing camera 开关、Horizontal crop（50%）、Vertical crop（0%）
- **画面比例（canvas）**：1:1 / 4:5 / 9:16 / 3:4 / 2:3 / 3:2 / 16:9 七种，覆盖社交平台主流封面
- **变换控件**：Size、Rotate X/Y/Z、Depth、Position XY、Reset、Lock / Hide
- **3D 基础物件**：Sphere / Cube / Torus / Cylinder / Cone / Disc / Gem / Arch 八种，每种可移动 / 旋转 / 重染色 / 删除
- **场景预设（12 个）**：Papercut · Retro · Chrome · Wireframe · Tiles · Ribbon · Orbit · Cloud · Circles · Brutalist · Steps · Architectural
- **设备组合预设**：Side by side（双机）· Phone + tablet（手机+平板）· Three screens（三屏）· Overlap（重叠）
- **设备光照预设**：Soft studio · After hours · Citrus · Blue hour · Rose · Close up
- **资产管理**：用户可保存自己的 preset（"My presets"）
- **上传限制**：图片 20 MB / 视频 100 MB · 视频时长上限 60 秒
- **文件隐私**：官网 hero 文案明示 "Your files stay on your device"

## 评论区反馈（事实摘录，不评价）

- **Charlie Clark（Maker，开帖）**："sliick turns your screenshots and videos into 3D device mockups, with everything running in your browser. Upload your screen, pick a scene, then make it yours: arrange the shapes, change the colors, set the camera, and add a little motion. Export a still image or an animated video when you're happy with it. It's 100% free, with no account, no watermark, and no uploads to a server."
- **Alexandra Protsenko（7h）**：Free with no watermark and no account is not something you see much anymore. If media never leaves the device, does that mean video export renders right in the browser too? I make a lot of launch screenshots so this looks handy.
  - **Charlie Clark 回复（5h）**：Yes, exactly. The scene is Three.js running on WebGL in your tab, and video export records that canvas directly in the browser (MediaRecorder under the hood). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. You get an MP4 (H.264) in browsers that support it, otherwise WebM, up to 1080p and 60 seconds.
- **Rohit（@rohthebuilder，2h）**：I have used this tool to get some better-feeling screenshots for my upcoming app. Really loved the simplicity of the tool, just a small recommendation to add "export to clipboard" as another export option.
  - **Charlie Clark 回复（37m）**：great feedback!
- **Aleksei Sharuk（HARPA AI，2h）**：Hey Charlie, congrats on the launch. Free status page for sliick.co, already recording uptime: https://upcontrol.io/status/sliick-co UpControl also ships as one npx package for AI coding agents...

- **Curious Kitty（PH，9h）**：A lot of people default to Rotato (or After Effects) for 3D mockup videos—what's the clearest quality/workflow point where Sliick wins, and where do you intentionally *not* try to compete with those heavier tools?
  - **Charlie Clark 回复（5h）**：Great question. Where Sliick wins is time to a finished shot. There's nothing to install, no account, no render queue. Upload a screen, pick a scene, nudge the camera and colors, export. For the 80% case (a hero image, a launch post, a store screenshot, a short looping clip) that's usually a couple of minutes instead of an afternoon.
